## 📖 Overview

**Craven** is an intelligent nutrition and grocery planning application that combines scientific dietary calculations with practical household budgeting. It builds personalized 7-day meal schedules tailored to your biometric profile, fitness goals, and regional tastes while cutting food costs through strategic ingredient reuse and zero-waste planning. It also features a multimodal AI food and label analyzer to evaluate dishes and packaged food nutrition on the fly.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **📋 Precision Diet Wizard:** Calculates BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor), TDEE, BMI, and customized calorie/macro targets (Protein, Carbs, Fats, Fiber) tailored to your goals (fat loss, muscle gain, maintenance, diabetes/PCOS care).
- **🌍 Flexible Dietary Preferences:** Supports Indian Vegetarian, Vegan, Eggetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Jain, Keto, High-Protein, and custom diets with budget tiers (**Thrifty**, **Balanced**, **Gourmet**) in **₹ (INR)**, **$ (USD)**, **€ (EUR)**, or **£ (GBP)**.
- **📅 7-Day Meal & Grocery Schedule:** Complete daily coverage for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks with macro breakdowns, prep times, and print-ready formatting.
- **♻️ Zero-Waste & Smart Ingredient Reuse:** Minimizes grocery bills and food spoilage by planning multi-day ingredient reuse (e.g., shared lentils, greens, grains) and providing batch-prep recommendations.
- **🔍 Multimodal AI Food & Label Analyzer:** Upload photos of food nutrition labels (OCR via Gemini Vision) or type any dish with portion sizes to get an instant **Craven Health Score (0–100)**, Glycemic Index rating, nutritional breakdown, and cost-effective healthy swaps.
- **🍳 Recipe Explorer & AI Chef:** Discover recipes via the Spoonacular API or instantly generate custom healthy recipes using Google Gemini.
- **🛡️ Dual-Engine Reliability:** Powered by Google Gemini AI with built-in procedural generation and verified food databases for seamless offline/fallback support.
